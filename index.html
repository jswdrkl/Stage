A Stage

Life in the entire universe is like an ever-continuous stage performance. Always live. Happening. This vast ‘stage’ is subdivided into so many small stages. These can be broadly classified as entire humanity > nations > towns > communities > homes > families…

On each such ‘stage’, every performance is a live performance. Impromptu. Unrehearsed. In fact, rehearsal is NOT possible. The actors thereupon continue to, rather have to perform from the moment they enter into their respective role till the time they exit.

The irony is - any individual actor simply cannot decide on his/her respective entry or exit timing. Not only that it is also not possible to choose the stage, co-actors, supporting actors, unknown actors, everything else and their respective acts, roles and performances; nothing. Whatever it is, it is.

Here, every individual actor has to act, no choice; because ultimately this entire ‘universal life stage’ is on an ever-running belt called TIME. Always moving ahead. Thus, an act once performed is performed. No backward correction is ever possible.

❁ ❁ ❁

All the above may sound depressing. But it is not that ruthless.

❁ ❁ ❁

If, during the course of play, any individual actor identifies, realises and understands ‘what’ went wrong for him/her, then he/she can do some ‘conscious fine tuning’ whenever, in future, the role demands to act in somewhat similar circumstances. This may also be deciding not to act at all, at that point of time. The earlier the understanding is, the better it is.

So, the only thing that is in the hand of the individual actor to keep his/her own performance as easy-going and stress free as possible is ‘awareness’. Because on this ever-moving-forward-in-time stage, only an aware actor can make ‘Suo-motu’ corrections in his/her role-play based on OWN experiences that are gathered by just ‘being aware’. Again, all this can be done ONLY for the role-playing of self. Not for others.

So, it is the ‘self-awareness’ that matters. And the easiest way to increase it is to adhere to ‘healthy habits’ as wholeheartedly as one can.
